# Page topology

One viewport sized canvas. No navigation, sections, scrolling, forms, or modal overlays. Fixed text snippets accumulate across the page and overlap. External citations are the only links.

Interaction model: time driven character by character typing at roughly 44.4 ms per character. New excerpts are randomly selected, sized and placed when the previous excerpt finishes. The three excerpt categories are diary notes, italic pink snippets, and pink underlined citations. The corpus resets after a category runs nearly empty. Citation links open a new tab. The cursor is hidden.
